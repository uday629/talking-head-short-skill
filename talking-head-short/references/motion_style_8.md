A second, independently-ranked pass over the same territory as the MOTION STYLE GUIDE tab in the genre sheet.
Where it names a mechanic `look.py`/`engine.py` didn't implement yet, that mechanic was added — see the `family → engine support` column.

**Rule:** a style family is a motion *system*, not a card type. Two videos in the same family should still pick different cards from that family's list and rotate layouts/transitions — the family fixes the *mechanics* (stroke weight, glow, grain, tilt, boil, stepped-time, shake, icon style), not the specific diagram.
